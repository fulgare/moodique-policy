Privacy Policy

Application: Moodique · Effective Date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Moodique ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the Moodique Android application.

1. Information We Collect

Moodique is designed to keep your personal entries and preferences lightweight and private:

  • User Content & Preferences: Entries, settings, and customization options you create in the app are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly export or share them yourself.
  • No Account Required: You can use Moodique without creating a user account or submitting personal registration details.

2. Data Storage, Export, and Deletion

Your data remains under your control on your device. Exporting, importing, or resetting local data happens only when you initiate those actions. Uninstalling the app removes app-managed local storage according to Android system behavior.

3. Third-Party Services & Permissions

Moodique only uses Android system features required for its core functionality. If you download or purchase the app through Google Play, Google may process standard store, billing, or diagnostic information under its own Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Moodique does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the developer contact listed on the Moodique Google Play store page or through the project repository.
